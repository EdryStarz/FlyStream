# FlyStream Lab
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web, local operator control and OBS browser output

## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, Canvas and existing Three.js fly/anatomy. A loopback Node.js service owns shared character state, persistence, official KICK adapters and optional AI/TTS credentials. Demo requires no external service.

## Users and purpose
Operators building a fictional fly channel, participating viewers and learners inspecting models. M0XA reacts to approved content and creates activities while `/science` preserves the instrument.

## Operating context
Desktop broadcast control with responsive mobile inspection. `/control` operates, `/stream` outputs, `/debug` explains decisions. Explicit start/resume arms output. OBS encodes broadcasts; capture requires an open operator tab.

## Capabilities and constraints
Local reactions, voice abstraction, rights-declared queue, demo events/chat, official KICK adapter, bounded memory, two scored games, local polls, cosmetics, sponsors, separate ledgers, markers and explicit vertical video recording. Relay/recording have no audio. No native KICK polls, semantic vision, transcription, cash processing, automatic publication or unattended reliability guarantee.

## Evidence and principles
SOURCE is actual selected pixels; MODELLED is optical/temporal processing; INFERRED is dimensionless response; MEASURED remains unavailable. VFB anatomy is structural evidence. CHARACTER is entertainment. Missing audience metrics remain unavailable. Demo is labelled and separate from real money. Secrets stay server-side; restart remains unarmed.

## Brand commitments
Underground biology laboratory and broadcast instrument: restrained colour, clear typography, separators, visible states and preservation of the scientific workstation.
