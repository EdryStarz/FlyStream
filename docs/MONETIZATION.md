# Monetization

The MVP delivers reactions/accounting surfaces, not checkout or guaranteed income. Demo tips/subscriptions/follows are labelled and separate from real ledgers. Real subscriptions are event counts; prices/revenue are never guessed.

Tips have minimum amount, cooldown and moderated celebration. User chat is not unrestricted TTS. The real ledger can represent verified events, but no actual cash-payment webhook provider is implemented. Demo controls do not verify payment.

Currencies remain separate, without guessed exchange rates. The habitat goal separates matching-currency demo/real progress; verified real progress can unlock the night room. Supporter wall/cosmetic celebrations give no competitive game advantage. Not every requested paid perk is delivered.

The dashboard summarizes available runtime totals and estimates; dedicated 7/30-day financial windows are not implemented. Subscription revenue, retention, watch time, revenue/viewer and conversions need authoritative inputs/denominators and remain unavailable. Participants are not concurrent viewers; correlation does not prove purchase causation.

Sponsors provide visible AD disclosure, campaign windows, capped scheduling and links. The form starts a one-hour campaign with five-minute intervals, fifteen-second slots and maximum six displays/hour. Counts are local slot displays, not audience impressions; link counts are local interactions. Follow [Compliance](PLATFORM_COMPLIANCE.md) for platform disclosure.

Affiliate links can use disclosed sponsor slots without viewer-profile targeting. Affiliate settlement, digital storefronts and automated recurring paid perks are not implemented. See [KICK](KICK.md) and [Operations](OPERATIONS.md). Reconcile actual provider records before reporting external revenue.
